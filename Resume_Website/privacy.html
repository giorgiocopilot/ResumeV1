Privacy

Privacy notice

This website is a personal, interactive résumé. It is designed to respect your privacy by default.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies are set.
  • No analytics, tracking pixels, fingerprinting, or third-party advertising scripts are used.
  • No personal data is collected from you automatically, and nothing is sent anywhere without your explicit action.

Contact buttons

The WhatsApp and email buttons simply open your own WhatsApp or email application with a pre-filled message. You decide whether to send it. If you choose to contact Giorgio, the information you send (such as your name, email address, and message) is processed only to respond to your enquiry, on the lawful basis of your voluntary request and legitimate interest in professional correspondence.

Fonts

This page loads typefaces from Google Fonts, which may process your IP address to serve the font files. If you prefer to avoid this, the site can be configured to self-host fonts. Contact Giorgio to request this.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal data you have voluntarily sent. To exercise these rights, or to withdraw consent to further correspondence, email george@copilotadoption.uk.

Data retention

Any correspondence you send is retained only as long as necessary to handle your enquiry and is then deleted, unless a longer period is required for legal reasons.
